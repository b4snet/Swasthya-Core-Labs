# ADR-0002: Proprietary Commercial Licensing

- **Status:** Accepted (placeholder; legal review pending)
- **Date:** 2026-09-16
- **Owner:** Legal / Architecture

## Context

Swasthya Core Labs is intended as proprietary commercial software. An
appropriate repository license was required that does not suggest
open-source availability.

## Decision

- The repository is licensed as **proprietary commercial software, all
  rights reserved** (`LICENSE`).
- The current `LICENSE` is a **placeholder** for a written commercial
  license agreement and must be finalized by legal counsel before any
  distribution, sale, or external use.
- Third-party components keep their own licenses; attribution commitment in
  `NOTICE.md`.

## Consequences

- **Positive:** unambiguous proprietary posture; no accidental open-source
  grant.
- **Negative:** placeholder license has no legal force until finalized —
  tracked as a legal review item.
- Contribution model: maintainer-led; external contributions only under a
  written contributor agreement (`CONTRIBUTING.md`).
