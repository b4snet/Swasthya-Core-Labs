# ADR-0006: Immutable Clinical Observations (Append-Only)

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-16
- **Owner:** Architecture / Clinical Informatics

## Context

Silent overwrite of clinical observations is a fundamental integrity risk.
Laboratory regulations and audit expectations require that original results
and provenance remain retrievable after correction.

## Decision

- Clinical observations and finalized clinical records are **immutable and
  append-only**.
- Corrections/amendments create **new versions** linking to prior versions;
  the original remains.
- Provenance (who/what/when/how, device/operator, units, timestamps,
  specimen chain) is retained per observation and cannot be retroactively
  edited.
- Amendments and corrections are distinct, separately audited flows.

## Consequences

- **Positive:** integrity alignment with ISO 15189/17025 data-integrity
  expectations; clean audit model.
- **Negative:** persistence design must support append-only and version
  resolution (schema decisions in Phase 1); simple in-place UI updates are
  prohibited by design — acceptable trade.
