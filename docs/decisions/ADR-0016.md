# ADR-0016: Master-Data Lifecycle and Append-Only Versioning

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-17
- **Owner:** Architecture

## Context

Clinical catalog definitions (tests, panels) change over time and their
historical definitions must be preserved. Phase 1 established append-only
audit, xmin optimistic concurrency, and immutability conventions
(ADR-0006, ADR-0011, ADR-0012).

## Decision

- **Two-tier model:**
  - **Mutable identity rows** (`tests`, `panels`) hold the stable code, the
    lifecycle `Status`, the `CurrentVersionNumber` pointer, and `xmin`.
  - **Immutable, append-only definition rows** (`test_versions`,
    `panel_versions`) hold the clinically significant definition fields and
    effective windows. They are never updated once persisted.
- **Lifecycle:** `Draft → Active → Inactive → Retired`, validated by a small
  permitted-transition matrix (`MasterDataLifecycle`). Transitions are explicit
  operations and are audited.
- **Versioning:** changing any definition field publishes a new version and
  advances the identity row's current pointer. Panels reference tests, never
  duplicate them; membership is ordered by a deterministic sequence number and
  may be revised only within the panel's identity/version model.
- **Concurrency:** `xmin` optimistic concurrency; conflicting writes surface as
  HTTP 409.
- **Deletion:** no convenience hard- or soft-delete for master data; `Retired`
  is the terminal state. Phase 1 deletion and immutability conventions remain
  authoritative.
- **Audit:** create/modify/activate/deactivate/retire operations are recorded
  through the Phase 1 allowlisted audit mechanism.

## Consequences

- **Positive:** historical definitions are preservable, reviewable, and
  auditable; a later result phase can reference the exact definition version in
  force.
- **Negative:** more tables and explicit version-publication logic; lifecycle
  transitions and concurrency must be validated and tested.
- See ADR-0006, ADR-0011, ADR-0012, ADR-0015.
