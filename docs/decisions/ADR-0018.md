# ADR-0018: Result-Data-Type and Reference-Interval Foundation

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-17
- **Owner:** Architecture / Clinical Informatics

## Context

The test catalog must describe the kind of result a test yields and carry
reference-interval structure, without implementing a result engine,
interpretation, clinical rules, or reporting (Phase 2 scope control; ADR-0006,
ADR-0008).

## Decision

- **`ResultDataType`** enumerates supported result kinds as catalog metadata:
  `Quantitative`, `Qualitative`, `Coded`, `Categorical`, `Ordinal`, `Boolean`,
  `Textual`, `Structured`, `PanelComponent`. This describes _definitions_; it is
  not a result engine and does not assume numeric-only results.
- **`ReferenceRange`** stores **structure only**: scope (organization/facility),
  test, a required provenance/source, optional population/sex/age dimensions,
  specimen context, low/high or textual reference, unit, and an effective
  window. **No clinical values are populated or invented**, and no
  interpretation or abnormality logic exists.
- **Definitions are distinct from results.** No patient results, observations,
  orders, accessioning, or reporting are implemented in this phase.
- Reference ranges and result data types are configuration, consistent with
  ADR-0008; authoritative content arrives in a later, governed phase.

## Consequences

- **Positive:** a configured, auditable basis for future result handling; the
  datatype model is explicitly not numeric-only.
- **Negative / deferred:** reference-interval selection and interpretation
  engines, clinical content, and critical-value workflows are future phases.
- See ADR-0006, ADR-0008, ADR-0016.
